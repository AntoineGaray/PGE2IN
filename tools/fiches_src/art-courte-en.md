## China timeline

| Dynasty | Dates | Keyword | Work or art | Idea to use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Huang Di, Xia | around 2700 BC, 2000-1600 BC | Myths | The Yellow Emperor's mausoleum | 5000 years of Civilization, *Zhong Guo* = the Middle Kingdom |
| **Shang** | 1600 – 11th c. BC | Writing | Oracle bones, bronzes, jade | A writing still readable today |
| **Zhou** | 1050 – 256 BC | The longest | Bronze, lacquers, painting | **Mandate of Heaven**, Warring States, **Confucius** (meritocracy), **Lao Zi** (Taoism, Yin and Yang) |
| **Qin** | 221 – 206 BC | The first emperor | **Terra Cotta Warriors Army**, Great Wall | Centralization, legalism, standardized writing, capital Xianyang |
| **Han** | 202 BC – 220 AD | Han culture | Silk, pottery, tomb paintings, tea | **Silk Road** (Emperor Wu), state Confucianism, Hanzi, Hanfu, arrival of Buddhism |
| Six Dynasties | 220 – 589 | Division | — | — |
| **Sui** | 581 – 618 | Reunification | **Grand Canal** | They repeat the Qin |
| **Tang** | 618 – 907 | Golden age | Painting, sculpture | Silk Road secured |
| **Song** | 960 – 1279 | Printing | Theatre | Defeated by the Mongols |
| **Yuan** | 1279 – 1368 | The Mongols | **White and blue ceramic**, novels | Kublai, Marco Polo, painters isolate themselves from power |
| **Ming** | 1368 – 1644 | The last Han rulers | **Forbidden City**, Great Wall, blue and white, *Journey to the West* | Hongwu, **Yongle**, **Zheng He**, trade with Europe, fall due to dependency on silver |
| **Qing** | 1644 – 1912 | The Manchus | **Peking Opera** | Kangxi, Qianlong (400 million inhabitants), isolationism (Canton), **Opium Wars** (1839-1842, 1856-1860), Cixi, Xinhai revolution |
| Republic (ROC) | 1912 – 1949 | Sun Yat-sen | — | Civil war, Japan in Manchuria (1931) |
| **PRC** | 1949 – | Mao, then opening, then Xi | Propaganda art, **Yue Minjun** | **Cultural Revolution** (1966-1976), soft power, cultural nationalism, control back since 2013 |

## Korea

Goryeo (918-1392, Buddhism, celadons). Joseon (1392-1897, Confucianism, the Hangeul alphabet). Japanese colony (1910-1945). Korean War (1950-1953). Soft power: *Gangnam Style*, K-pop, cinema.

## India

Indus Valley Civilization. Vedic period (the Vedas, the birth of Buddhism). Maurya Empire (Ashoka). Delhi Sultanate and the Mughals (**Taj Mahal**). East India Company (17th c.). British Raj. Independence with **Gandhi** (1947). McDonald's cultural adaptation.

## The course's cross-cutting ideas

- **Power and legitimacy**: the Mandate of Heaven, state Confucianism, the Yamato lineage.
- **Opening and closure**: the Silk Road (Han, Tang, Yuan) versus Qing isolationism and the Edo closure.
- **Global trade**: silk (Han), ceramics (Ming), silver, opium.
- **Religions**: Confucianism, Taoism, Buddhism (from India via the Silk Road), Shinto.
- **Soft power today**: manga and Nintendo, K-pop, Korean cinema, Chinese science fiction, the Hanfu trend.

## Traps

- The Yuan are Mongol, the Qing Manchu: they are **not Han dynasties**.
- Confucianism remains the state ideology until the fall of the Qing in **1912**, in the 20th century.
- The Taj Mahal is **Mughal** (17th century).
