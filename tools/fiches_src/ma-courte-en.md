The 4 calculations that come up: **total and unit cost, BEP, indifference threshold, full costing with cost centres**. Every result needs a sentence of interpretation.

## Formulas

| What | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Total cost | TC = FC + Q × uVC |
| Unit cost | TC / Q (falls as Q rises) |
| High-low | v = Δcosts / Δunits, then F = TC − v × Q |
| Contribution margin | CM = TO − VC = Q × uCM, with uCM = SP − uVC |
| Profit | PM = CM − FC |
| CM ratio | CM / TO = 1 − uVC / SP |
| BEP in units | Q\* = FC / uCM, **rounded up to the next unit** |
| BEP in revenue | FC / CM ratio = Q\* × SP |
| BEP date | Q\* / annual sales × 12 months |
| Target profit | Q = (FC + target profit) / uCM |
| Margin of safety | Sales − sales at the BEP |
| Indifference threshold | Q = (FC1 − FC2) / (uVC2 − uVC1) |
| Full cost | DC + allocated IC, PM/u = SP − full cost/u = MDC/u − IC/u |
| Unit cost driver | uCD = centre IC / total CD units |
| Absorption product cost | DM + DL + Var MOH + Fixed MOH / units **produced** |
| Variable costing product cost | DM + DL + Var MOH |

## Definitions to know

- **3 types of companies**: service (no inventory), merchandising (1 inventory), manufacturing (3 inventories: raw materials, WIP, finished goods).
- **3 manager responsibilities**: planning (the budget), directing, controlling.
- **Financial accounting**: external, past, mandatory standards. **Management accounting**: internal, future, no standards, decision-oriented.
- **Value chain**: R&amp;D, design, purchases/production, marketing, distribution, customer service.
- **Cost object**: anything whose cost we want to know (product, customer, project, department).
- **Direct cost**: traced directly. **Indirect cost**: allocated.
- **Variable cost**: total proportional to volume, constant per unit. **Fixed cost**: constant total, falls per unit. **Mixed cost**: y = vx + F.
- **Inventoriable cost**: DM + DL + MOH, held in inventory then expensed as COGS when sold. **Period cost**: everything else (R&amp;D, selling, admin, distribution).
- **MOH**: indirect materials, indirect labor, other plant costs.
- **Absorption costing**: fixed MOH in inventory, subtotal = gross margin, mandatory externally. **Variable costing**: fixed MOH expensed in the period, subtotal = contribution margin, a decision tool.
- Production &gt; sales: absorption income &gt; variable income (the difference is fixed MOH/u × change in inventory).
- **Cost driver**: a variable correlated with IC consumption, preferably physical.
- **Homogeneous**: resources are consumed in stable proportions, so the choice of CD changes nothing. **Heterogeneous**: the result depends on the CD, and the true cost does not exist.
- **Cross-subsidization**: one product pays for another's IC.
- **3 allocation methods**: simple allocation (one CD), organisational cost centres (auxiliary then main, two steps), ABC (resources, then activities, then products).
- **ABC in 4 steps**: identify activities, assign IC to them, choose the CDs, allocate to products.

## Decision tree: "drop a product?"

1. PM &gt; 0: keep it.
2. PM &lt; 0 but MDC &gt; 0: **keep it**, the problem comes from IC allocation.
3. MDC &lt; 0 but CM &gt; 0: **keep it**, act on price, volume and direct costs.
4. CM &lt; 0: **drop it**.
