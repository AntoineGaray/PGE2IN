The exam mixes **MCQs, open-ended questions and a business case study**. The MCQs test the dates and figures below; the case is handled with the donut, ISO 26000 and CSR maturity.

## Definitions

- **Sustainable development (Brundtland, 1987)**: meeting the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
- **CSR (ISO 26000)**: the responsibility of an organization for the impacts of its decisions and activities on society and the environment. Ethical and transparent behaviour that contributes to sustainable development, takes stakeholders' expectations into account, complies with the law and is integrated throughout the organization.
- **CSR (European Commission, 2011)**: the **voluntary** integration of social and environmental concerns. The CSRD adds: identify, prevent, report and go beyond regulatory compliance.
- **Global Compact**: 10 universal principles (human rights, labour, environment, anti-corruption).
- **Regeneration**: going beyond reducing and neutralising impacts to generate **net positive impacts**. The company works like a tree.
- **Weak sustainability**: capitals are substitutable. **Strong sustainability**: natural capital is irreplaceable (Planet, then People, then Profit).

## Figures for the MCQs

- **Climate**: CO2 at 280 ppm in 1850, limit at 350 ppm, **426 ppm in 2025**, critical threshold at 450 ppm. **+1.1°C** already (+1.2°C over 2012-2021).
- **Biodiversity**: **25%** of species threatened, **35%** of pollinators gone in France, **75%** of land altered, extinction **100 to 1,000 times** faster, the 6th mass extinction. The 5 causes: land use change, exploitation, climate, pollution, invasive alien species.
- **Planetary boundaries**: **7 out of 9 crossed**, interdependent, with tipping points.
- **SDGs**: **17 goals, 169 targets, 244 indicators**, adopted by 193 states, voluntary.
- **Summer 2026 in France**: drought over €5.6 billion, €10 to 12 billion of losses in total, 42,000 hectares burnt at Saumos.
- **Plastic**: about 5 g a week, a credit card.
- **80%** of a product's environmental impact is determined at the design phase.

## The donut

- **Ceiling**: the 9 boundaries. Novel entities, stratospheric ozone depletion, atmospheric aerosol loading, ocean acidification, biogeochemical flows, freshwater change, land-system change, biosphere integrity, climate change.
- **Floor**: the 12 social floors. Food, health, education, income & work, water, energy, networks, housing, gender equality, social equity, political voice, peace & justice.
- **Between the two**: the safe and just space. It brings an integrated vision, takes social and environmental issues out of the "externalities" category, and proposes metrics beyond GDP.

## ISO 26000: the 7 central questions

1. Organizational governance
2. Human rights
3. Relations and working conditions
4. The environment
5. Fair practices
6. Consumer issues
7. Communities and local development

The standard is **voluntary and non-certifiable**: companies use labels such as AFNOR "Engagé RSE" or Lucie 26000. The diagnosis is done question by question: what the company does, its weaknesses, its evidence, then a level (Insufficient, Intermediate or Advanced).

## The 4 levels of CSR maturity

1. **Defensive**: compliance.
2. **Corrective**: optimisation, reducing costs.
3. **Strategic**: CSR integrated into the strategy.
4. **Transformative or Regenerative**: CSR drives the business model, with a positive net impact.

## Action plan

- **The ERC sequence + R**: avoid, reduce, offset (as a last resort), regenerate.
- **Along the whole value chain**: procurement, design, operations, logistics, use and end of life, compensation.
- **The 4 regenerative pillars**: net positive impact, biomimicry, systemic vision, shared governance.
- **The 4 steps (CEC)**: understand your pressures, question the need, set a 2030 course, choose 3 to 5 levers.
- **The risks**: physical, transition, reputational.
